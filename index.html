How disputes work

Plain language, no fine print. The full rules live in the contract itself.

  • ⚖️

    Decided by AI Judge

    Any dispute goes to an AI Judge, shown the evidence only. Judgment is final — there is no appeal.

  • 💰

    Disputing costs a bond

    The payer posts a bond of 20% of the job (min $1, max $50) with the dispute. The bond follows the ruling: if the judge awards the worker half or more, the bond goes to the worker; otherwise it returns to the payer.

  • 🤝

    Settle it yourselves

    While a job is disputed, the payer and worker can settle at any amount they both agree to — one party signs, the other submits. The bond returns to the payer and no judge is needed.

  • ⏱️

    72-hour safety valve

    If no ruling executes within 72 hours of a dispute, anyone can call the refund and the job amount plus the bond return to the payer in full — so funds can never lock forever.

  • 🛡️

    Payer can't claw back

    A split needs the worker's signature for the exact amount — the payer can't take funds back unilaterally. Splits below 1% of the job amount revert as dust. The worker can also deliver and dispute atomically in one transaction, so the payer can't front-run the dispute. After the window, the payer can no longer dispute or split — the worker can claim the full amount, or the payer can still release it in full.
