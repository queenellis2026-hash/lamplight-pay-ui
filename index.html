How disputes work

Plain language, no fine print. The full rules live in the contract itself.

  • 🗝️

    The arbiter team

    Three wallets hold dispute keys. A ruling needs 2 of 3 to agree on the exact amount — one arbiter alone can't move anything.

  • 🔄

    Atomic rotation

    Arbiters change only when 2 of the current 3 sign off-chain and both signatures land in a single transaction. No proposal window, so nobody can front-run a change.

  • 🚫

    Conflict rule

    The contract blocks an arbiter from being the payer or worker on a job. An arbiter can't rule on their own job — it's enforced in code, not policy.

  • ⏱️

    72-hour window

    After delivery and the review window, disputes get 72 hours. If the arbiters can't agree on an exact amount in time, the worker is paid in full — so funds can never lock forever.

  • 🛡️

    Payer can't claw back (V14)

    A split now needs the worker's signature for the exact amount — the payer can't take funds back unilaterally. Splits below 1% of the job amount revert as dust. The worker can also deliver and dispute atomically in one transaction, so the payer can't front-run the dispute. After the window, only the worker can claim, or a dispute decides.

Governance

Arbiter-only. Rotation and token changes are atomic: two current arbiters sign off-chain, anyone submits both signatures in one transaction. No front-running possible.

How you're protected

  • 🔒

    Locked, not sent

    Payment stays in the contract until the job settles. Nobody can take it mid-job.

  • 🔑

    Four ways funds move

    Payer releases · worker claims after the review window · 2-of-3 arbiter vote · 72h timeout pays the worker.

  • ⚖️

    Disputes expire

    Either side can dispute a delivery. Our team reviews and rules within 72 hours. Silence pays the worker.

  • 📜

    Public record

    Every job, delivery, and ruling is a transaction you can open on the explorer.
