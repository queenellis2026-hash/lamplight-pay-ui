🧭 Guided demo — try the full flow

Six steps, two wallets, about five minutes. This is the same flow the BOT Chain criteria ask for: multiple wallets, multiple on-chain interactions, every step visible on the explorer.

1.Connect a wallet — click Connect wallet above. MetaMask will ask to add BOT Chain Testnet (chain 968) if you don't have it.
2.Get test gas — click Get free tBOT (gas), paste your address at the BOT Chain faucet ↗, complete the check, claim. Gas is free on testnet.
3.Get test dollars — click Get free test dollars in your wallet card. These are play-money mUSDC for the demo.
4.Create a job — enter a worker's address, a payment amount, and what needs doing. Step 1 — Allow the payment, then Step 2 — Create the job. Two on-chain transactions.
5.Play the worker — connect a second wallet (or use the worker address): Accept job → Mark as delivered (describe the delivery; it's sealed on-chain as a hash).
6.Release the payment — back as the payer, click Release payment. The worker gets paid. Click any address link to see it all on the BOT Chain explorer ↗.

Tip: open the page in two browsers (or a normal + private window) to play payer and worker side by side.

⚖️ Disputes — the trust layer

This is what makes escrow trust infrastructure instead of just a payment rail. If a delivery is wrong, either side can raise a dispute while the job is Delivered and the review window is still open. The job's arbiter then has 7 days to rule: pay the worker, or refund the payer. If the arbiter goes silent, either party can settle after the deadline — the money defaults to the worker, so a dispute can never freeze funds forever.

Note: jobs created here set no arbiter, so the payer acts as arbiter. Pass an arbiter address at creation time (contract-level) to use a neutral third party.
